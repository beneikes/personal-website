Services

Control design
Current, speed and position control, including sensorless schemes such as HFI and LQG-based estimation.
Embedded firmware
Turning control algorithms into firmware that runs reliably on the target.
Motor characterization
Careful measurement and identification of motor parameters as the basis for good control.
Hardware-in-the-loop
HIL systems for testing drive controllers before they meet real hardware.
Bring-up and troubleshooting
A second set of eyes on a difficult bring-up, across control, firmware, electronics and mechanics.
