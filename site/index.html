Motor control engineering · Cologne, Germany

Electric drives that run well, from control algorithm to hardware.

I'm Benedikt Neikes, a control systems engineer (M.Sc.) with more than ten years of experience designing motor-control systems end to end. I help teams in automotive, robotics and manufacturing get their drives working, and keep them working.

Book a call b.neikes@cosyne.de

01 Services

Control design
Current, speed and position control, including sensorless schemes such as HFI and LQG-based estimation.
Embedded firmware
Turning control algorithms into firmware that runs reliably on the target.
Motor characterization
Careful measurement and identification of motor parameters as the basis for good control.
Hardware-in-the-loop
HIL systems for testing drive controllers before they meet real hardware.
Bring-up and troubleshooting
A second set of eyes on a difficult bring-up, across control, firmware, electronics and mechanics.

02 About

My work spans the whole drive: the control algorithms, the firmware, the electronics, and the mechanics that tie them together. I have a soft spot for the hard problems, and I like building things that work, then writing down how they work so others can build on them.
