Services

  • System identification

    Your simulation and the real system behave differently, the datasheet is incomplete or wrong, or you need a substitute motor or want to benchmark a competitor’s. Reliable parameters are the basis for analysis and control design; I measure them with specialized test benches, drives, and algorithms.

    • Parameter identification for BLDC, PMSM, IPMSM, SynRM, and stepper motors
    • Linear parameters (Ld, Lq, ψm, Rs) and nonlinear flux maps
    • Inertia, elasticity, friction, and backlash of your mechanism or gearbox

  • Control design

    Your drive doesn’t reach its targets for dynamics, efficiency, or noise, has to run without a position sensor, or earlier attempts haven’t delivered.

    • Modeling, analysis, and simulation of the drive system in MATLAB/Simulink or Python
    • FOC with MTPA, field weakening, and SVPWM
    • Sensorless control (silent HFI, back-EMF observers)
    • Torque, velocity, and position control: cascaded P/PI, PID, or state-space (adaptive LQG, adaptive feedforward control)
    • Automatic code generation with Embedded Coder (MISRA C compliant)
    • Hand-written C code for efficient implementation
    • Peripheral configuration and driver implementation
    • MIL/SIL/PIL validation

  • Custom hardware

    Off-the-shelf drives can’t do the job, or you need specialized test equipment.

    • Drive electronics development
    • Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) test benches with real-time load emulation
    • Test systems for automated end-of-line (EOL) testing
    • Test systems for parameter identification and validation

  • Troubleshooting

    Your system misses its design goals or vibrates, your sensorless control fails and the cause isn’t obvious, or some units run fine while others fail.

    • Design reviews and hands-on support during development
    • Commissioning of new motors, drives, and actuators
    • Root-cause analysis of instability, noise, and start-up issues
    • Tuning and optimization of existing control systems

  • Training

    You want to develop your drives in-house, but the know-how and toolchain are still missing. You’re looking for a foundation your engineers can build on long after the project ends.

    • Workshops on FOC, sensorless control, and model-based design
    • Toolchain setup and coaching for engineers

Available by the hour or day, or at a fixed price for scoped tasks. For longer projects, I can join your team as a full-time contractor on-site.

About

I’m Benedikt Neikes, a control systems engineer based in Germany. After studying medical engineering (B.Sc.) and mechatronics (M.Sc.), I’ve spent nearly ten years as an independent engineer, developing motor control solutions for automotive, aerospace, robotics, and manufacturing clients.

My approach is model-based, so algorithms and parameters are grounded in physics rather than empirical tuning. From advanced control design and efficient code implementation to hardware development and validation, I lead projects from concept to production.

Over the years, I’ve helped companies build up their own development capabilities. Working closely with engineering teams and stakeholders, and sharing what I know along the way, is the part of my work I enjoy the most.
